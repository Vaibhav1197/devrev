# How to Store Your PRD in Notion or Google Docs

We have provided the PRD in two formats:
1. **Markdown Format:** [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md)
2. **Rich Formatted HTML:** [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.html`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.html)

---

### Option A: Storing in Notion (Recommended)

#### Method 1: Direct File Import (Cleanest & Fastest)
1. Open [Notion](https://www.notion.so) in your browser or desktop app.
2. In the left sidebar, click **"Import"** or navigate to any page and click the `•••` (Page options) in the top-right corner.
3. Select **"Import"** > **"Markdown & CSV"**.
4. Choose the file: [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md).
5. Notion will automatically convert headers, tables, callouts, and bullet points into native Notion blocks!

#### Method 2: Copy-Paste
1. Open [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md) in your editor.
2. Select all (`Ctrl + A` or `Cmd + A`) and Copy (`Ctrl + C` or `Cmd + C`).
3. Paste directly into a blank Notion page. Notion will parse the markdown formatting automatically.

---

### Option B: Storing in Google Docs (Google Drive)

#### Method 1: 1-Click Copy via HTML (Preserves All Table Styles & Colors)
1. Double-click to open [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.html`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.html) in your browser (Google Chrome / Edge).
2. Press `Ctrl + A` (Select All) and `Ctrl + C` (Copy).
3. Open a new document at [docs.new](https://docs.new).
4. Press `Ctrl + V` (Paste).
5. All colored badges, shaded table headers, callout boxes, and typography will be pasted cleanly into Google Docs!

#### Method 2: Google Drive File Upload
1. Open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **"+ New"** > **"File upload"**.
3. Upload [`PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md`](./PRD_Offline_Mode_Mobile_Order_Processing.md).
4. Right-click the uploaded file in Google Drive and select **"Open with"** > **"Google Docs"**.
5. Google Docs will convert the markdown directly into a editable document.
