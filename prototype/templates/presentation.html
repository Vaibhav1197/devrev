01 / Problem Scenario & Objective

DevRev SupportIQ: Customer Support & Defect Triage Portal

🏢 Real-World Customer Context

DevRev Solutions Engineers build rapid prototypes and demo environments to showcase how unified CRM, support, and engineering workflows resolve customer friction.

  • Field Failure Scenario: Enterprise wholesale clients (e.g. Apex Wholesale, $420k ARR) operate in cellular dead zones (basements, walk-in freezers), experiencing checkout timeouts and duplicate order mutations.
  • The Core Problem: Traditional support ticketing platforms (Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud) function as isolated silos where customer tickets sit disconnected from development issue trackers.
  • Business Risk: Delayed root-cause diagnosis leads to customer escalations, breached SLAs, and severe contract churn risks.

🎯 Prototype Mission & Deliverables

Build a responsive, modern full-stack web application that demonstrates cohesive system thinking across customer operations and product engineering:

  • Record Lifecycle: Create, view, search, filter, and mutate support tickets with status transitions (Open → In Progress → Resolved).
  • Revenue-Aware Operations: Track financial impact (ARR at risk) directly against active P0/P1 blockers.
  • Agentic AI Triage: Automate ticket summarization, sentiment extraction, and intelligent linking to Jira/DevRev engineering work items.

03 / Data Model & Schema

Entity Relationships & Core Data Attributes

📋 Primary Entity: records (Support Ticket)

  • id (INTEGER PK) & ticket_key (Unique human identifier, e.g., TICK-101)
  • title & description (Issue summary & technical reproduction steps)
  • customer_name & customer_tier (Enterprise, Mid-Market, Growth)
  • priority (P0-Blocker → P1-High → P2-Medium → P3-Low)
  • status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
  • impact_revenue (Financial contract value tied to escalation)
  • ai_sentiment, ai_summary, ai_recommended_action

📜 Audit Entity: activity_logs

Tracks an immutable audit trail for every mutation to support compliance and post-incident investigation:

  • id (INTEGER PRIMARY KEY)
  • record_id (Foreign Key referencing records.id)
  • action (CREATED, UPDATED, STATUS_CHANGE)
  • details (Diff of mutated attributes, e.g. Status: 'Open' → 'In Progress')
  • actor (e.g. Solutions Engineer, Customer, or AI Agent)
  • timestamp (ISO-8601 UTC timestamp)
04 / Working Demo Walkthrough

Demonstrating Core Product Workflows

1. Ingestion & Real-time Filtering

Live search queries across ticket keys, titles, and customer names with 250ms debouncing. Instant filtering by Status and Priority with dynamic KPI recalculations (Open, In Progress, P0 Blockers, Revenue at Risk).

2. Record Creation (POST /api/records)

Modal dialog capturing customer name, tier, category, initial priority, and business impact with automatic key generation (e.g. TICK-106) and atomic database commit.

3. Status Mutation & Audit Trail (PUT /api/records/:id)

One-click status buttons transition records across the lifecycle while writing immutable diff entries into the audit log timeline.

4. Interactive AI Triage (POST /api/records/:id/ai-triage)

Tapping "Re-Run AI Triage" dynamically parses technical descriptions, identifies sentiment, produces an executive summary, and suggests exact Jira work item links (e.g. OM-102)!

05 / System Thinking & AI Integration

How DevRev & Modern CRM Systems Leverage This Data

🧩 1. Automated Defect Clustering & Deduplication

DevRev's AI agents ingest hundreds of disparate customer tickets and cluster similar symptoms (e.g. "504 checkout timeouts" + "dead zones") under a single parent Work item (Jira OM-101 / PRD v3.4).

💰 2. Predictive Churn & Revenue Risk Modeling

Aggregating impact_revenue across open P0 blockers alerts Account Executives when high-value accounts ($420k ARR Apex Wholesale) reach dangerous SLA breach thresholds.

🔄 3. Automated "Close the Loop" Notifications

When an engineer merges PR #251 in GitHub and closes Jira issue OM-102, DevRev automatically notifies all affected customer contacts that their duplicate order bug is fixed in v3.4.

🤖 4. Agentic AI Support Co-Pilot

AI scans past incident RCAs, API design specs, and PRD documents to suggest instant resolution steps directly to customer support engineers.
